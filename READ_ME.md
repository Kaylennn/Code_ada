WHAT WILL THIS APP DO
Web-based geospatial map that tracks real, reported citizen-science data of invasive species
Educational aspect to teach people about invasive species (plants and pests); people get points for each educational section they finish
Maybe each button you click also gives you information/education about that species and proper ways of mitigation — ways to sustainably get rid of the pests
People can also report invasive species that they see for points and possibly win prizes
Farmers can report invasive species patches - application has buttons with pictures of key invasive species which farmers select the one they see
Once many reports of a species in a certain area, it creates a cluster and depicts a “volunteer pull site” and calculates approximately how many volunteers and hours are needed to clear the invasive species
Specifically help farmers by prioritizing invasive plants that harm farms, or patches that are closer to farms
Maybe also list AI generate a list of the contact info. of nearby volunteer groups/ organizations focusing on this issue
